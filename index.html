    How to listen

    • Sit or lie down comfortably. Close your eyes or rest your gaze on the circle.
    • Breathe with the circle: in for four counts, out for six.
    • Rest your attention on the part of the body where each center sits as its tone plays. You can repeat its seed sound silently.
    • Headphones give the fullest sound and turn on the headphone beat. On a phone, add it to your home screen from the browser menu to use it like an app, even offline.
