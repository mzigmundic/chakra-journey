# Chakra Sound Journey

A guided tone meditation through the seven chakras: a low drone and singing-bowl tones tuned to each chakra, with a circle to breathe along with.

**Open it:** https://mzigmundic.github.io/chakra-journey/

- Full journey: 7 chakras × 5 minutes, root to crown
- Single chakra: 2, 5, 10 minutes or no limit
- All sound is generated in the browser, nothing is downloaded
- Installable: on Android, open the page in Chrome and choose *Add to home screen* / *Install app*. After the first visit it works offline.

Files: `index.html` (the app, fonts built in), `manifest.webmanifest` (install info), `sw.js` (offline support), `icons/`.

Fonts: Marcellus, Figtree and IBM Plex Mono (SIL Open Font License), via Fontsource.
